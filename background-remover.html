What Background Remover is for

Background Remover is designed for cutting a person, object, product, or subject out of an image. It can keep the subject on transparency or place the subject on a new filled background.

  • Create transparent PNG cutouts.
  • Place subjects on a white background.
  • Place subjects on a solid color background.
  • Place subjects over another background image.
  • Adjust how aggressively the background is removed.
  • Brighten or darken the kept subject.
  • Use edge feathering to soften harsh cut lines.
  • Use Protect Regions when too much of the subject is removed.
  • Use Refine Regions for difficult local areas like hair, faces, shoulders, or hands.
  • Use Subject Selection when you only want one person or one area.
Local-first note: Background removal works as a desktop workflow in Grafiq where supported. You do not need to upload private photos to an online background remover.

How to remove a background in Grafiq

  1. Open Background Remover.
    In Grafiq, open More → Background Remover.
  2. Add images.
    Click Add Images…, or drag and drop supported images into the file list if your build supports it.
  3. Choose an output folder.
    Click Choose Output Folder…, or leave the default output location if you are just testing.
  4. Choose an output format.
    Choose PNG if you want transparency. Choose JPG if you want a normal image with a filled background.
  5. Choose a background mode.
    Use Transparent, White, Solid Color, or Background Image.
  6. Preview the result.
    Click Preview to run background removal using the current settings.
  7. Adjust the main controls if needed.
    Use Background Removal, Subject Brightness, and Edge Feather to improve the result.
  8. Fix problem areas if needed.
    Add a Protect Region if part of the subject is disappearing. Add a Refine Region if one area needs a smarter second pass.
  9. Use Subject Selection if needed.
    Add a Subject Selection if you only want one person or one area from the image.
  10. Apply to all.
    When the preview looks good, click Apply to All to export the queued images using your current settings.

Choosing a background mode

  • Transparent: Keeps the subject on transparency. Best with PNG.
  • White: Places the subject on a clean white background.
  • Solid Color: Lets you choose a background color.
  • Background Image: Places the cutout over another image.
Simple rule: Use PNG + Transparent when you want a cutout. Use JPG + White when you want a normal image with a simple filled background.

How to improve difficult cutouts

Background removal depends on the image. Some photos work perfectly on the first preview, while others need a few adjustments. Hair, dark clothing, low contrast, shadows, cluttered backgrounds, or similar subject/background colors can make the result harder.

  • Background Removal: Lower values keep more of the subject. Higher values remove more aggressively.
  • Subject Brightness: Brightens or darkens the subject after the background is removed.
  • Edge Feather: Softens the edge slightly. Small values usually look more natural.
  • Protect Region: Use this when clothing, hair, hands, or body parts are getting removed too aggressively.
  • Refine Region: Use this when one local area needs a second pass, such as hair, a face, shoulders, or a low-contrast edge.
  • Subject Selection: Use this when you want to isolate only one person or one specific area from the result.

Protect Region vs. Refine Region vs. Subject Selection

  • Use Protect Region when the remover is cutting away part of the subject that should stay.
  • Use Refine Region when one difficult area needs a smarter second pass.
  • Use Subject Selection when you only want one person or one specific part of the image exported.
Clean workflow: Preview first. If too much subject is removed, lower Background Removal or add a Protect Region. If one area still looks wrong, add a Refine Region and preview again. If you only want one subject, use Subject Selection.

Good uses for Background Remover

  • Create product photos for listings.
  • Make profile or avatar cutouts.
  • Prepare thumbnails or simple graphics.
  • Place a subject on a white or solid background.
  • Create transparent PNGs for presentations or documents.
  • Extract one person or subject from a photo.
  • Make quick image composites without opening a large design app.

What to watch out for

  • Background removal results vary by image. Some photos work well immediately, while others need adjustment.
  • JPG does not preserve transparency. Use PNG for transparent cutouts.
  • Hair, shadows, dark clothing, and low-contrast edges may need Protect or Refine Regions.
  • Too much Edge Feather can make the subject look soft.
  • Too aggressive Background Removal can cut away parts of the subject.
  • Busy backgrounds may need more manual refinement.
  • Test one image before running a large batch.
