Privacy-first by design

Ranbyte Apps tools are designed to avoid unnecessary accounts, ads, tracking, and cloud dependencies. When an app can do useful work locally, that is the preferred design.

Grafiq processes files locally by default and does not send your files to Ranbyte Apps. The only exception is if you choose to email or share files with us for support.
