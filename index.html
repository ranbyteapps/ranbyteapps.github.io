What Grafiq does

  • Convert images and supported videos between common formats
  • Resize, crop, downscale, and upscale media files
  • Remove photo backgrounds where supported
  • Create GIFs, MP4 slideshows, and motion effects
  • Edit, search, import, and export metadata
  • Redact private information with blur, pixelate, boxes, highlights, and labels
  • Find exact or near-duplicate images
  • Organize files by folder, type, date range, extension, and rename patterns
  • Split scanned photo sheets into separate image files
  • Preview, edit, and export SVG files
  • Use video tools for trimming, rotation, audio overlays, and picture-in-picture overlays

Grafiq is designed like an old-school utility: choose your files, choose a tool, export the result.
