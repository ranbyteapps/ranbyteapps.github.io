Create motion from still images with zoom, pan, and parallax

Use Grafiq Motion Effects to turn still images into simple motion clips with zoom, pan, frame blend, and parallax-style movement.

What Motion Effects is for

Motion Effects is useful when you want to turn a still image into a short video or animated result. Instead of a static photo, you can create movement with zoom, pan, frame blending, or depth-style effects.

  • Create smooth zoom effects from still images.
  • Create pan-style motion across an image.
  • Create depth or parallax-style movement where supported.
  • Create frame blend effects for a softer motion look.
  • Create creative motion variations from one source image.
  • Preview motion before exporting when available.
  • Export motion results as new files without changing the original image.
Local-first note: Motion Effects works on your computer. You do not need to upload personal photos to an online animation or motion-effect service.

How to create a simple zoom or pan effect

  1. Open Motion Effects.
    In Grafiq, open More → Motion Effects.
  2. Choose an image.
    Select the photo, scan, screenshot, or graphic you want to animate.
  3. Choose a motion type.
    Pick a motion effect such as smooth zoom, pan, or another available motion option.
  4. Set the duration.
    Choose how long the motion clip should be.
  5. Adjust the motion amount.
    Use the available controls to make the movement subtle or stronger.
  6. Preview the result.
    Preview the motion before creating the final export.
  7. Export the motion clip.
    Save the result as a new file.
Simple rule: Subtle motion usually looks better than extreme motion. A slow zoom or gentle pan can make a still photo feel polished without distracting from the image.

How to use depth or parallax-style motion

Depth-style motion can make a still image feel more dimensional by shifting parts of the image in a way that suggests foreground and background movement. This type of effect works best on images with a clear subject and background separation.

  1. Choose a photo with a clear subject.
    Photos with a person, object, or strong foreground/background separation usually work best.
  2. Open Motion Effects.
    Add the photo to Motion Effects.
  3. Choose the depth or parallax option.
    Select the available depth/parallax-style motion effect.
  4. Keep the movement moderate.
    Smaller movement often looks more natural than exaggerated movement.
  5. Preview carefully.
    Watch edges, faces, and background details to make sure the motion still looks acceptable.
  6. Export the result.
    Save the motion clip as a new file.

Good images for Motion Effects

  • Family photos with a clear subject.
  • Product images or object photos.
  • Landscape photos with room to pan or zoom.
  • Old scanned photos that need a little movement for a slideshow.
  • Wide images that can be panned across slowly.
  • Images with enough resolution to crop or zoom without looking too soft.

Motion effect ideas

  • Slow zoom in: Good for portraits, product photos, and family photos.
  • Slow zoom out: Good for revealing more of a scene.
  • Pan left or right: Good for wide landscapes or group photos.
  • Depth parallax: Good for photos with a strong foreground subject.
  • Frame blend: Good for a softer, smoother creative look.

Good uses for Motion Effects

  • Create short motion clips from still photos.
  • Add life to old family photos for a slideshow.
  • Create gentle movement for a video intro or background.
  • Make a still product image feel more dynamic.
  • Create simple media inserts for Video Tools.
  • Make short animated clips without using a full video editor.

Tips for better results

  • Start with the highest-resolution image you have.
  • Use subtle motion for a cleaner result.
  • Avoid zooming too far into low-resolution images.
  • Preview the motion before exporting.
  • Check faces, edges, and text after motion effects.
  • Keep the original image unchanged and export a new motion version.

What to watch out for

  • Motion effects cannot create true video footage from a still image.
  • Strong zooms can make the image look soft or pixelated.
  • Depth or parallax-style effects may look better on some images than others.
  • Photos with busy backgrounds, fine hair, text, or thin edges may show artifacts with stronger effects.
  • Always preview the final motion before using it in another project.
